There was no website, which meant there was no single authoritative place to point at when
someone asked what Next Step Studios is, or what it has built. That's now fixed.

The site is deliberately small and static. Every page is generated ahead of time and
served as plain HTML, with no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no analytics. Fonts are
served from the site itself rather than a third-party font CDN. The practical consequence
is that there is almost nothing to maintain, nothing that can quietly collect information,
and nothing that needs a monthly bill.

Two decisions shaped the content. Projects are listed with the status they've actually
reached, and the set of allowed statuses doesn't include anything that would let an
unfinished project read as a release. And the bot's policies get stable addresses here,
clearly labelled as drafts, so that the documents can be linked to without implying they
are already in force.

What comes next is the thing this site can't do for the Studio: finishing and verifying
the work behind those statuses.
