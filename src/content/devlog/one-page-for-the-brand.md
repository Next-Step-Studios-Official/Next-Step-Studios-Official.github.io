The brand was spread across three pages: `/press` for the boilerplate and logo files,
`/brand` for the rules, and `/archive` for the marks that had been replaced. Each page was
reasonable on its own. Together they made a visitor guess which one held the thing they
needed, and they gave the same logo three different addresses to drift apart from.

They are now one page at `/press`, called **Press & brand**. It carries the boilerplate,
quick facts, every current file, the palette, the typefaces, the usage rules, the retired
neon emblem and the superseded white-tile edition. A section nav stays at the top, and the
complete kit is still one ZIP.

## The old addresses still work

`/brand` redirects to the guidelines section and `/archive` redirects to the archive
section. That matters because links to those pages exist outside this repository: old
posts, documents and search results. A consolidation that breaks its own history is just a
different kind of archive problem.

The files themselves did not move. Every logo keeps the same `/brand/press/...` or
`/brand/archive/...` path. The ZIP's README now points at the new page rather than the old
routes.

## Small corrections that came with it

The team roster was still presented as five people even though four entries were
placeholders. The About and Team pages now label those cards as examples, and the public
copy no longer turns an unconfirmed roster into a fact. The count is still described as the
intended roster on the team page, where the context is explicit.

The old logo entry also claimed the SVG files were real vectors. They are not: they embed
the transparent PNG at each size. The brand page now says that plainly, and the old entry
carries a correction instead of quietly rewriting what was published.

## What else changed

The header, footer, search index, command palette, sitemap and Step now point at one brand
destination instead of three. A new [Now](/now) page records what is actually in progress
this month. The home page gives the project and the bot the first actions rather than
sending people to the policy shelf first.

None of the policy text changed. This was a website and navigation pass, not a legal one.
