## The idea

Most of what the studio builds starts as a repeated task: checking a document set, preparing a
release, keeping one source of truth in several formats. An app is the next step after the
scripts — something with an interface that does not require opening a terminal.

That is the whole idea. There is no chosen platform, no design, no feature list and no name.

## What is decided

Only that an app is a direction worth keeping visible. The studio would rather publish four
honest categories than quietly imply a product is coming.

The Discord bot remains the current work. The website and its documents are the public
reference. Games are the longer-term direction. An app sits alongside the studio tools and will
be started only when there is a specific job it does better than a script.

## What is not decided

- the platform — desktop, mobile or web;
- the first feature, or whether it would be released publicly;
- a title, an icon or a date;
- whether it would be free, paid or internal only.

If the entry ever moves past Planned, this page will say what changed and what actually exists.
Until then, nothing here is available to download or try.
