## What Places Left Behind is

**Places Left Behind** is an independently developed first-person 3D exploration and mystery
game. It is being developed by **Next Step Studios** entirely from scratch using **C#** and a
**custom game engine**. It is not being developed in Unity, Unreal Engine or Godot.

The engine uses **Vulkan** for graphics and **Silk.NET** for the .NET bindings.

## Explore, photograph, piece it together

The player explores abandoned locations, discovers clues, examines documents, photographs
interesting evidence and gradually uncovers the stories behind forgotten places.

The game focuses on:

- exploration;
- environmental storytelling;
- mystery;
- investigation;
- photography and clue collection;
- documents and evidence;
- atmospheric environments;
- discovering what happened in abandoned locations.

There is no combat or fighting. The intended experience is immersive and atmospheric, built
around curiosity and discovery rather than action.

## How a session works

The game is planned to include:

- first-person exploration;
- a camera for photographing discoveries and clues;
- documents and other evidence;
- a home base or office where players can review photographs, documents and collected
  information;
- equipment preparation;
- multiple explorable locations;
- environmental storytelling and mysteries.

The initial demo concept includes two small locations, with a target experience of roughly
**30–60 minutes per location**. Those are development goals, not promises about release content.

## Technology

- **C#** and **.NET 10**.
- A **custom game engine**.
- **Vulkan** for graphics.
- **Silk.NET** for the .NET bindings.
- **Blender**.
- A **glTF 2.0 / GLB** asset pipeline.

The engine is being developed independently of the game so that it can potentially become a
separate product in the future.

**C# is pronounced “C-sharp”.** The `#` is the musical sharp sign, not a hashtag, so it is not
“C hashtag”. “From scratch” means the game's code and engine are being written for this project
rather than assembled from a ready-made game kit or an existing game engine.

## Where development is now

This is an **early prototype**.

The player can currently move using **WASD** and control the camera with the mouse. The project
has basic engine, windowing, Vulkan initialization, input, scene, entity, transform and player
foundations.

Substantial work remains. The game is not finished, and the current prototype is not a completed
playable game. The actual environment-loading pipeline, full rendering system, investigation
mechanics, complete locations and much of the gameplay are still under development.

Planned functionality is not described here as already implemented. There is no release date,
trailer or finished game to announce.

## Release and distribution

No release date or commercial distribution platform has been committed to. Future distribution
may happen through the Next Step Studios website or other storefronts, but no particular
storefront has been chosen.
