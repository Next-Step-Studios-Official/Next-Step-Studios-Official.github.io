## What is decided

Games are the direction. The studio was founded to make them, and the Discord bot is
the work that funds the time to learn how.

## What is not decided

Everything else: the title, the genre, the engine, the platform, the art direction and
the date. None of those have been chosen, and none will appear here until they have.

## When this page changes

The status moves to **Prototype** only when something runnable exists — a build that
can be played, even badly. Until then, "Planned" is the accurate word, and adding
detail would be decoration rather than information.
