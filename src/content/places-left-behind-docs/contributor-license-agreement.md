## 2. Background

The project consists of a proprietary game engine and a proprietary game. The owner may license the engine separately in future, possibly under an open-source licence. To preserve that option, the owner needs sufficient rights in every contribution.

This agreement grants those rights. It does **not** transfer ownership of the contributor's work.

## 3. Definitions

- **"Contribution"** means any code, asset, documentation, design, test, or other material the contributor submits to the project, in any form, including through a pull request, patch, message, or file transfer.
- **"Project"** means the Places Left Behind game, its engine, its documentation, and related materials.
- **"Owner"** means Candler Shaw.

## 4. Ownership Retained by the Contributor

1. The contributor retains copyright and all ownership rights in their Contribution.
2. This agreement does not assign copyright to the Owner and does not make the Owner a co-author.
3. The contributor may use, license, and distribute their Contribution elsewhere, including in other projects, subject to section 7.4.
4. The contributor may grant other licences to their Contribution, provided those grants do not conflict with the rights granted here.

## 5. Licence Granted to the Owner

The contributor grants the Owner a **perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, transferable licence** to:

1. use, reproduce, and store the Contribution;
2. modify, adapt, and create derivative works of the Contribution;
3. combine the Contribution with other material;
4. distribute the Contribution and derivative works in source and binary form;
5. sublicense these rights through multiple tiers of distribution;
6. **relicense** the Contribution under any licence the Owner chooses, including a proprietary licence, the MIT License, the Apache License 2.0, a BSD licence, or a commercial licence;
7. enforce the rights granted here.

This licence is granted so that the Project, and any part of it, can be licensed on terms chosen by the Owner, including terms different from the ones under which the Contribution was received.

## 6. Patent Licence

The contributor grants the Owner and recipients of the Project a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive patent licence to make, use, sell, offer for sale, import, and otherwise transfer the Contribution, limited to patent claims the contributor can license that are necessarily infringed by the Contribution alone or in combination with the Project as submitted.

If the contributor initiates patent litigation alleging that the Project infringes a patent, the patent licence granted here for that Contribution terminates as of the date the litigation is filed.

## 7. Contributor Representations

The contributor represents and warrants that:

1. **Originality.** The Contribution is their original work, or they have sufficient rights to submit it under this agreement.
2. **Authority.** They have the legal right to grant the licences in this agreement.
3. **Third-party material.** Any third-party material included is clearly identified, and its licence permits the use intended by the Project. A list of such material and its licences is included with the Contribution.
4. **No conflicting obligations.** The Contribution is not subject to an employment agreement, contractor agreement, or other obligation that conflicts with this agreement.
5. **No malware.** The Contribution contains no malicious code, hidden functionality, or undisclosed network communication.
6. **Accuracy.** They have not knowingly included material that infringes another person's rights.

## 8. Third-Party Material

1. Material the contributor does not own must be disclosed before submission.
2. The Owner may decline a Contribution that includes material with an unclear or incompatible licence.
3. A Contribution must not include material that would require the Project to disclose its source code or to adopt a licence the Owner has not chosen.
4. Attribution required by a third-party licence must be supplied with the Contribution.

## 9. Moral Rights

1. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the contributor waives, or agrees not to assert, moral rights in the Contribution against the Owner and the Project's users, including the right to be identified and the right to object to derogatory treatment.
2. Where moral rights cannot be waived in the contributor's jurisdiction, the contributor consents to the acts described in section 5, including modification and relicensing, and agrees not to assert moral rights in a way that interferes with them.
3. The Owner will credit contributors in the project's records and, where practical, in the game's credits, unless the contributor asks not to be credited.

## 10. No Obligation, No Compensation

1. The Owner is not obliged to accept, merge, use, or maintain any Contribution.
2. No payment, royalty, equity, or compensation is owed for a Contribution unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.
3. Submitting a Contribution does not create an employment, contractor, partnership, or agency relationship.

## 11. Record Keeping

1. The Owner will record each accepted Contribution, the version of this agreement accepted, and the date.
2. The record supports future licensing decisions, including relicensing the engine.
3. The contributor may request confirmation of what has been recorded about their Contribution.

## 12. Withdrawal

1. Once granted, the licence in section 5 cannot be revoked for Contributions already merged into the Project.
2. A contributor may ask for their Contribution to be removed from future versions. The Owner will consider the request in good faith, but is not obliged to remove it if the Contribution is embedded in the Project.
3. A contributor may stop contributing at any time.

## 13. Disclaimer

The contributor provides the Contribution "as is", without warranty of any kind, to the extent permitted by applicable law, except for the representations in section 7.

## 14. Governing Law and Capacity

1. The intended jurisdiction is the State of Georgia, United States, together with applicable federal law. This is subject to legal review and confirmation; no venue or dispute-resolution mechanism has been selected.
2. The Owner is currently under 18. Capacity to enter into this agreement, and the need for guardian involvement, must be resolved before it is used.
3. A contributor who is a minor may require guardian acceptance for this agreement to be effective.
4. If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue, and the unenforceable provision is limited to the minimum extent necessary.

## 15. Entire Agreement

This agreement is the entire agreement about the Contribution. It may be amended only in writing, and an amendment applies only to Contributions made after it takes effect unless the contributor agrees otherwise.

## 16. Points Requiring Legal Review

1. Whether a licence-based CLA of this breadth is enforceable as drafted.
2. Whether the relicensing grant in section 5.6 is adequately clear.
3. Whether the patent clause and its termination trigger are proportionate and enforceable.
4. The treatment of moral rights in the contributor's jurisdiction.
5. Whether a signature, click-through, or commit sign-off is required for validity.
6. Whether a separate corporate CLA is needed for contributions made in the course of employment.
7. Capacity and guardian issues for both parties.
8. Whether the "as is" disclaimer in section 13 is appropriate given the representations in section 7.
