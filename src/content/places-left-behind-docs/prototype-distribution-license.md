## 1. Purpose

This license sets out the terms under which an invited tester may access and use a prototype build of Places Left Behind, if and when the owner begins distributing prototypes.

It is intended to let testers genuinely evaluate the game — including talking about it, sharing a reasonable amount of footage, and reporting problems — while keeping control of unreleased builds and unreleased content.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Owner"** means Candler Shaw, the individual who owns Places Left Behind and operates under the public-facing name Next Step Studios.
- **"Prototype"** means any pre-release build of Places Left Behind, in any form, provided by the owner to the tester, including executables, data files, shaders, assets, and configuration.
- **"Tester"** means the individual to whom the owner has given access to a Prototype.
- **"Confidential Material"** means non-public information about the Prototype and the project, including builds, source code, unreleased content, planned features, internal documentation, and private communications from the owner. It does not include material the owner has published, material the tester independently developed, or general impressions and opinions.
- **"Feedback"** means bug reports, suggestions, comments, and other information the tester provides about the Prototype.

## 3. Limited Permission to Access and Use

Subject to the tester's compliance with this license, the owner grants the tester a limited, personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable permission to:

1. install and run the Prototype on hardware the tester owns or controls, for the purpose of testing and evaluation;
2. play, explore, and examine the Prototype for that purpose;
3. create screenshots and gameplay recordings of the Prototype, subject to section 8;
4. provide Feedback to the owner;
5. discuss the Prototype publicly within the limits in section 9.

No other rights are granted. This permission is not a sale, not a transfer of ownership, and not a license to the game, the engine, or any asset.

## 4. Restrictions on Redistribution

The tester must not, and must not permit anyone else to:

1. copy, upload, share, publish, or otherwise distribute the Prototype, in whole or in part, to any person who has not been given access by the owner;
2. post download links, file-sharing links, mirrors, torrents, or archives of the Prototype or any part of it;
3. include the Prototype in any compilation, bundle, or distribution service;
4. sell, rent, lease, sublicense, or otherwise commercially exploit the Prototype;
5. share access credentials, private download links, or invitation codes with anyone else;
6. stream, broadcast, or publish the Prototype's files themselves, as distinct from footage of gameplay.

## 5. Restrictions on Modification

The tester must not:

1. modify, patch, decompile, disassemble, or reverse engineer the Prototype, except to the extent that applicable law expressly permits and that permission cannot be excluded by agreement;
2. create or distribute modified builds, "unofficial patches", cracks, trainers, or derivative versions;
3. extract assets, models, textures, audio, writing, or code from the Prototype for use outside it;
4. use the Prototype's content to train, build, or improve another product;
5. remove, obscure, or alter any copyright, attribution, or build-identification notice.

Configuration files that the game itself exposes for settings may be adjusted by the tester in the ordinary way. That is not a modification for the purposes of this section.

## 6. Confidentiality

The tester should treat Confidential Material as private.

1. The tester must not disclose Confidential Material to any third party, and must not publish source code, internal documents, private messages, or unreleased story content.
2. The tester should not publicly describe specific unreleased story details, endings, or clue solutions in a way that spoils the game for future players. General impressions and discussion of what the prototype contains are acceptable.
3. This section does not prevent the tester from: discussing the Prototype's existence; giving honest opinions; criticising the game; reporting bugs publicly where the owner has invited it; or disclosing information that is already public, that the tester already lawfully knew, or that the law requires them to disclose.
4. The obligation attaches only to material actually received under this arrangement, and lasts while the information remains non-public. It is not a general gag order and does not restrict discussion of the tester's own experience.
5. The tester may keep a private record of their own Feedback.

## 7. Feedback and Bug Reports

1. Feedback is voluntary. The tester is not obliged to test anything, to test for any minimum period, or to produce any particular report.
2. Feedback should be submitted through the channel the owner designates. The owner may change the channel by notice.
3. The license the tester grants in Feedback is set out in the [Prototype Feedback and Bug Report Policy](/docs/places-left-behind/prototype-feedback-policy). In summary, the owner may use Feedback to develop the game without payment or obligation, and the tester keeps ownership of their own original expression.
4. The tester must not include third-party confidential information, personal data about other people, or material they have no right to share in a report.
5. The owner is not obliged to act on any particular report or to fix any particular defect.

## 8. Screenshots, Recordings, and Streaming

The owner does not impose a blanket ban on gameplay footage of the Prototype. Footage is useful for an early prototype.

**Permitted without asking:**

1. Taking screenshots and recording gameplay for personal use.
2. Publishing short gameplay clips, screenshots, and streams of the Prototype, including on video platforms and social media.
3. Commenting on, criticising, or reviewing the Prototype in text, audio, or video.
4. Monetising that coverage through ordinary platform revenue programmes, provided the coverage is not principally a re-upload of the game's content and does not distribute the Prototype itself.

**Conditions:**

1. Coverage must make clear that the game is an early prototype and that features may change.
2. Coverage must not present the Prototype as a finished or released product.
3. Coverage must not present modified builds, or footage of content the owner has not shown, as authentic.
4. Long-form, minimally transformative uploads of the game's content — such as full unedited playthroughs published as a substitute for playing — are not permitted without written permission.
5. Footage must not be used to advertise an unrelated product, or in a way that suggests the owner endorses it.
6. The owner may ask for specific spoiler-sensitive material to be held back before a public reveal. Such requests apply only to material identified as spoiler-sensitive and only for a stated period.

**Attribution:** Coverage should name the game as "Places Left Behind" and may name Next Step Studios. Attribution is appreciated and, for published coverage, expected.

## 9. Public Discussion and Reviews

1. The tester may discuss the Prototype publicly, including in forums, social media, and video.
2. Honest criticism is permitted and is not a breach of this license.
3. The tester must not state or imply that the Prototype is a final release, that the owner has endorsed their views, or that the tester speaks for the studio.
4. The tester must not publish Confidential Material as defined in section 6.
5. Reviews published after a public release are governed by ordinary law and by the released game's end-user terms, not by this prototype license.

## 10. Build Expiration and Replacement

1. Prototype builds are time-limited development snapshots, not products.
2. A Prototype may stop functioning after a stated date, after a stated number of launches, or when the owner withdraws it. The owner does not guarantee that a Prototype will continue to run.
3. The owner may replace a Prototype with a newer build at any time. When a build is superseded, the tester should stop using the superseded build and delete it.
4. Prototype saves may be incompatible between builds and may be lost. The owner is not responsible for lost progress.
5. When the testing programme ends, or when the owner asks, the tester should delete all Prototype copies in their possession, except for footage already published under section 8.

## 11. Removal of Access

The owner may suspend or end a tester's access at any time, with or without cause, and without notice where necessary.

Grounds may include breach of this license, redistribution of a build, harassment of other testers, or the end of the programme. Ending access does not affect any rights the owner has in respect of a breach that already occurred, and does not entitle the tester to a refund, payment, or credit, because access is provided free of charge unless a separate written arrangement says otherwise.

## 12. Ownership

1. The Prototype and everything in it remain the property of the owner and its third-party licensors.
2. The tester acquires no ownership, licence, or other right except the limited permission in section 3.
3. Third-party components in the Prototype remain subject to their own licenses and are attributed in the build or its accompanying notices.

## 13. No Warranty

THE PROTOTYPE IS PROVIDED "AS IS" AND "AS AVAILABLE", WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND. IT IS AN EARLY PROTOTYPE THAT MAY CRASH, LOSE DATA, CONTAIN INCOMPLETE FEATURES, AND CHANGE WITHOUT NOTICE. THE OWNER DISCLAIMS ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT.

## 14. Limitation of Liability

TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, THE OWNER IS NOT LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOSS OF DATA, PROFITS, OR GOODWILL, ARISING FROM THE PROTOTYPE OR THIS LICENSE, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY. NOTHING IN THIS LICENSE LIMITS LIABILITY THAT CANNOT LAWFULLY BE LIMITED, INCLUDING LIABILITY FOR FRAUD OR FOR DEATH OR PERSONAL INJURY CAUSED BY NEGLIGENCE WHERE SUCH LIABILITY CANNOT BE EXCLUDED.

## 15. Term and Termination

This license begins when the tester is given access to a Prototype and continues until the earlier of: the owner ending access, the tester deleting all Prototypes and notifying the owner, or the end of the testing programme. Sections on confidentiality, ownership, feedback licensing, warranty, liability, and governing law survive termination.

## 16. Changes to These Terms

The owner may revise these terms for future builds. A revision does not retroactively change the terms that applied to a build already provided, unless the tester accepts the revision. Material revisions should be dated and recorded in the revision history below.

## 17. Governing Law and Capacity

1. The intended jurisdiction is the State of Georgia, United States, together with applicable federal law. This is subject to legal review and confirmation; no venue or dispute-resolution mechanism has been selected.
2. The owner is currently under 18. Capacity to enter into this license, and the possible need for a parent or guardian to be a party or to consent, must be reviewed before this license is used. No attorney has reviewed it.
3. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in effect to the extent permitted by law.

## 18. Entire Agreement

This license, together with the [Prototype Tester Agreement](/docs/places-left-behind/prototype-tester-agreement) and the [Prototype Feedback and Bug Report Policy](/docs/places-left-behind/prototype-feedback-policy) if those are separately accepted, is the entire agreement about the Prototype. It replaces prior discussions on the same subject. It may be amended only in writing by the owner.

## 19. Open Items Requiring Confirmation

- Whether testers will be asked to accept these terms by click-through, signed agreement, or both.
- Whether any jurisdiction requires consumer-protection disclosures for free prototype access.
- Whether minors will be admitted as testers, and what guardian consent would then be required.
- Whether a dispute-resolution clause should be added.
- Whether builds will use time limits, launch limits, or a remote kill switch, and how that is disclosed.
