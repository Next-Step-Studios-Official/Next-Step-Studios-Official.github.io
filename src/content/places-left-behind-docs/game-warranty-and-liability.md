## 1. Purpose

This notice states, in plain language, the warranty and liability position intended for Places Left Behind. It accompanies the end-user terms and is meant to be readable without legal training.

It is a summary. Where it differs from the operative agreement, the operative agreement controls.

## 2. The Game Is Provided "As Is"

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Places Left Behind is provided **"as is"** and **"as available"**, without warranty of any kind.

This means the owner does not promise that:

- the game will run on your hardware, even if it meets published requirements;
- the game will be free of defects, crashes, or visual errors;
- the game will run without interruption;
- any defect will be fixed;
- the game will remain compatible with future drivers, operating systems, or hardware;
- saves will survive an update;
- modifications will continue to work;
- the game will receive further updates at all.

The game is an independent production in active development. It is not a finished, supported enterprise product.

## 3. What Is Not Warranted

The following implied warranties are disclaimed to the maximum extent permitted by law:

- merchantability;
- fitness for a particular purpose;
- satisfactory quality;
- accuracy;
- quiet enjoyment;
- non-infringement, in respect of the game as delivered;
- compliance with any particular standard or regulation.

## 4. Prototype Builds

Prototype and test builds carry stronger warnings than released builds.

- They are incomplete and may contain placeholder, missing, or broken systems.
- They may crash or corrupt progress.
- They may expire or stop working.
- They may be withdrawn.
- They are provided free of charge, unless a separate written arrangement says otherwise.

## 5. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the owner is not liable for:

- indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages;
- loss of profits, revenue, or business opportunity;
- loss of data, including save files and screenshots;
- loss of goodwill;
- damage to hardware or software arising from use of the game, where the owner has not caused it by negligence;
- costs of substitute goods or services;
- claims arising from modifications or third-party content.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, total liability is limited to the amount paid for the game, or to the minimum amount required by applicable law if that is greater. For free prototype builds, that amount is zero.

## 6. What Cannot Be Excluded

Nothing in this notice or the end-user terms excludes or limits liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited. Depending on the law that applies, this may include:

- liability for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation;
- liability for wilful misconduct or gross negligence;
- liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence;
- liability for breach of mandatory consumer guarantees.

Where a limitation is unenforceable in a particular jurisdiction, the remaining provisions continue to apply, and the unenforceable provision is limited to the minimum extent the law allows.

## 7. Consumer Rights

If you are a consumer, you may have statutory rights that cannot be waived by agreement. Nothing here affects those rights. In some jurisdictions you may be entitled to a remedy for a faulty product regardless of a disclaimer, and you may be entitled to bring a claim locally.

Because the intended jurisdiction is Georgia, United States, and the game may be sold elsewhere, these clauses need review against the consumer law of each market before release.

## 8. Third-Party Components

Third-party components included in the game carry their own warranty positions. The owner does not warrant third-party software, and does not pass on any warranty from its authors. Those components are provided under their own licences, which typically disclaim warranty as well.

## 9. Data Loss

Save files, screenshots, and configuration are stored on your system. You are responsible for backing them up.

The game does not currently transmit data anywhere, and no cloud backup exists. If cloud saves are added later, their behaviour and any associated warranty position must be described before the feature ships.

## 10. Reporting Problems

Problems should be reported through the published support channel. Reporting a problem does not create an obligation to fix it, and does not change the warranty position.

Security issues should be reported privately. See the [Community Reporting Policy](/docs/places-left-behind/community-reporting-policy) draft.

## 11. Legal Review Required

This notice requires review by a qualified lawyer before it is used. Specific points to review:

1. Whether the disclaimer is enforceable in the intended jurisdiction, in the form drafted.
2. Whether the liability cap is enforceable, and whether a cap of the purchase price is meaningful for a low-priced independent game.
3. Whether mandatory consumer guarantees apply in each target market.
4. Whether the treatment of minors as users requires additional steps.
5. Whether any statement about "non-infringement" creates unintended exposure.
6. Whether a separate notice is needed for the free prototype builds, given that no payment is involved.

The owner is currently under 18, so capacity and guardian involvement should also be considered before any warranty or liability term is relied upon.
