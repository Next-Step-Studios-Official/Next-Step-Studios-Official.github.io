## 2. Background

The owner is developing Places Left Behind, a first-person exploration and mystery game, together with a custom game engine. The game is an early prototype: the player can currently move and control the camera, and the rendering, world loading, investigation, photography, saving, audio, and interface systems are incomplete or not yet implemented.

The owner may invite a small number of individuals to test prototype builds. This agreement sets out the terms of that invitation.

## 3. What the Tester Is Being Offered

1. Access to one or more prototype builds, free of charge unless a separate written arrangement states otherwise.
2. A channel for submitting bug reports and feedback.
3. No payment, no equity, no employment, no contractor relationship, and no expectation of compensation.
4. No promise of continued access, of future builds, of a finished game, or of any particular feature.

Testing is a voluntary activity. The tester may stop at any time.

## 4. Terms Incorporated

By accepting this agreement, the tester also accepts the [Prototype Distribution License](/docs/places-left-behind/prototype-distribution-license) and the [Prototype Feedback and Bug Report Policy](/docs/places-left-behind/prototype-feedback-policy). If those documents and this agreement conflict, this agreement controls for the tester relationship, and the distribution license controls for the use of builds.

## 5. Access and Use

The tester may install and run prototype builds on hardware they own or control, for testing and evaluation, and may do the things permitted by the distribution license: play the game, take screenshots and recordings, share feedback, and discuss the prototype within the limits stated there.

## 6. Restrictions

The tester must not:

1. redistribute, publish, mirror, or share prototype builds, download links, or invitation codes with anyone;
2. sell, rent, or commercially exploit builds or their contents;
3. modify, decompile, or reverse engineer builds, except where applicable law expressly permits;
4. extract and reuse game assets, code, or writing outside the prototype;
5. publish source code, internal documents, or unreleased spoiler content the owner has identified as confidential;
6. harass other testers, the owner, or third parties;
7. misrepresent their relationship with the studio, or claim to speak for it.

## 7. Confidentiality

The tester agrees to keep Confidential Material private as described in section 6 of the distribution license. This is a limited confidentiality obligation. It does not prevent the tester from discussing the prototype publicly, giving honest criticism, or disclosing information that is already public or that the law requires to be disclosed.

## 8. Feedback

1. The tester may submit bug reports, suggestions, and opinions through the designated channel.
2. Feedback is voluntary, and the tester is not obliged to produce any.
3. The tester grants the owner the feedback licence described in the [Prototype Feedback and Bug Report Policy](/docs/places-left-behind/prototype-feedback-policy): a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, reproduce, modify, and incorporate the Feedback for developing and promoting the game, without obligation to the tester.
4. The tester keeps ownership of the original expression in their feedback. The licence is a permission to use it, not an assignment of the tester's work.
5. The tester confirms that feedback they submit is their own, or that they have the right to share it, and that it does not contain third-party confidential information or other people's personal data.

## 9. Gameplay Recording, Screenshots, and Public Discussion

The tester may record, publish, and stream prototype gameplay, and may publish screenshots, criticism, and reviews, subject to the conditions in sections 8 and 9 of the distribution license. The owner does not impose a blanket ban on footage of the prototype. Coverage should identify the game as an early prototype and should not present it as a finished release.

## 10. Build Expiration, Replacement, and Deletion

Builds may expire, be replaced, or stop working. Saves may be lost. When the programme ends or the owner asks, the tester will delete all copies of prototype builds in their possession, other than footage already published under section 9.

## 11. Credits and Attribution

The owner may credit testers in the game's credits or on the studio's website, but is not obliged to. The tester may ask to be credited under a preferred name, or to be left out. Crediting a tester does not give the tester any ownership of the game, and a credit is not a licence.

## 12. Removal of Access

The owner may end the tester's access at any time, with or without cause. Access is free, so ending it does not create a right to a refund or compensation. The owner may also withdraw an individual build or the whole programme.

## 13. Ownership

The prototype, the game, the engine, and all original content remain the property of the owner. The tester gains only the limited permission described in this agreement and the distribution license. Third-party components remain subject to their own licenses.

## 14. Independent Development

The tester may work on their own projects, including games. Nothing in this agreement restricts that, except that the tester must not use the owner's confidential material or extracted assets in those projects, and must not reproduce the game's unreleased story or content.

## 15. Privacy

The owner will hold the tester's name, contact details, and feedback for programme administration. The owner will not publish the tester's contact details without permission. Detailed handling is described in [`../Privacy/Game-Data-Policy.md`](/docs/places-left-behind/game-data-policy); note that no tester data is currently collected, because no programme exists.

## 16. No Warranty

THE PROTOTYPE IS PROVIDED "AS IS", WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED. It may crash, lose data, contain incomplete systems, and change without notice. The owner does not warrant that it will run on any particular hardware or that any defect will be fixed.

## 17. Limitation of Liability

TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, THE OWNER IS NOT LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, OR FOR LOSS OF DATA, PROFITS, OR GOODWILL, ARISING FROM THE PROTOTYPE OR THIS AGREEMENT. Nothing limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

## 18. Term and Termination

This agreement begins on the date of acceptance and continues until the earlier of: the owner ending access, the tester withdrawing, or the programme ending. The provisions on confidentiality, feedback licensing, ownership, warranty, liability, and governing law survive termination.

## 19. Capacity and Guardian Involvement

The owner is currently under 18. The tester may also be a minor.

- Where either party is a minor, this agreement may require a parent or legal guardian to accept the terms, and may be voidable or unenforceable without that involvement.
- Both parties should treat the agreement as subject to review before it is relied upon.
- The intended jurisdiction is Georgia, United States, subject to legal review and confirmation.

## 20. Governing Law and Disputes

The intended jurisdiction is the State of Georgia, United States, together with applicable federal law. No venue, arbitration clause, or other dispute-resolution mechanism has been selected. This must be resolved before the agreement is used.

## 21. Entire Agreement and Amendments

This agreement, the distribution license, and the feedback policy are the entire agreement about prototype testing. They replace earlier discussions on the same subject. Amendments must be in writing and, for a material change, accepted by the tester.

## 22. Acceptance

Acceptance must be recorded through a documented process — for example a dated click-through record, a signed copy, or a written confirmation retained by the owner. No acceptance record exists today, and no tester is bound by this draft.
