## 1. Purpose

These terms would govern content a creator deliberately submits for official hosting, featuring, or inclusion — as distinct from content the creator publishes independently.

They are written to grant the studio only what it needs to host and show the content, and nothing more.

## 2. What Counts as a Submission

- A Mod submitted to an official channel.
- Artwork, screenshots, or video submitted for a showcase.
- Writing submitted for a community page.
- A map or location submitted for official featuring.
- Any content sent in response to an official call for submissions.

Content posted independently — on a personal channel, a third-party site, or a community space the studio does not operate — is not a submission and does not become one by being noticed.

## 3. Who Can Submit

1. The creator of the content, or someone authorised by the creator.
2. A person who has the rights to everything included in the submission.
3. A person able to accept these terms. A minor would need guardian acceptance.

The studio may decline a submission without giving a reason.

## 4. Ownership

1. **The creator keeps ownership.** Submitting content does not transfer copyright, and does not make the studio a co-author.
2. The creator may continue to publish and license their content elsewhere, provided those licences do not conflict with the permission granted here.
3. The studio does not claim ownership of the underlying ideas, designs, or characters the creator originated.

## 5. Permission Granted to the Studio

By submitting, the creator grants the studio a **non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence** to:

1. host and store the submitted files;
2. display the content on official channels and in promotional material for the game;
3. distribute the content to users through official channels;
4. reproduce the content as needed for delivery, including format conversion and packaging;
5. use the content's title, description, and the creator's credited name to identify it;
6. create preview images and short excerpts for promotion.

This licence is:

- **limited** to hosting, display, distribution, and promotion through official channels;
- **non-exclusive** — the creator may distribute elsewhere;
- **revocable for future distribution** — the creator may ask for removal, and the studio will stop future distribution within a reasonable time;
- **not** a transfer of ownership;
- **not** a right to sell the content, relicense it, or use it in the studio's own products.

Using submitted content inside the game itself would require a separate written agreement.

## 6. Creator Warranties

The creator confirms that:

1. the content is their original work, or they have permission for everything included;
2. any third-party material is identified, with its licence;
3. the content does not infringe anyone's rights;
4. the content contains no malware or undisclosed network communication;
5. the content does not include another person's private information;
6. they have the authority to grant the licence in section 5.

## 7. Prohibited Submissions

- Content that redistributes the game's proprietary files or assets.
- Content including third-party material with an incompatible licence.
- Content that infringes copyright, trademark, or privacy.
- Content that sexualises minors, in any form, absolutely.
- Content promoting hatred against a group.
- Malware, downloaders, or hidden telemetry.
- Content that impersonates the studio or another creator.

## 8. Review

1. The studio would review a submission before publishing it.
2. Review would check for prohibited content, completeness, and basic functionality.
3. Review is not a quality guarantee and is not an endorsement.
4. The studio may request changes, publish as-is, or decline.
5. Review capacity may be limited, and delays are possible.

## 9. Credits

1. The studio would credit the creator by the name they choose.
2. The creator may ask to be credited differently or, where practical, omitted.
3. A credit is a courtesy and is not a payment or an ownership interest.
4. The studio may list credits on the official channel, in an in-game credits screen, or on the website.

## 10. Removal

The studio may remove a submission when:

1. it breaches these terms;
2. it infringes a third party's rights;
3. it is unsafe, malicious, or harmful;
4. a credible infringement claim is received;
5. the creator asks for removal;
6. the channel is discontinued.

Removal would not affect the creator's ownership, and would not entitle either party to compensation. Copies already obtained by users are outside the studio's control.

## 11. No Compensation

1. No payment, royalty, or revenue share is owed for a submission unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.
2. The studio does not acquire any obligation to promote, maintain, or keep hosting a submission.
3. Donations to a creator through a third-party platform are between the creator and that platform.

## 12. Version Updates

1. A creator may submit an updated version of their content.
2. The studio may host the newer version in place of the older one.
3. The creator is responsible for keeping a submission compatible with the current game version, or for marking it incompatible.

## 13. Privacy

A submission may include the creator's name and contact details. These would be used for administration and, if the creator agrees, for credit. Handling is described in [`../Privacy/Game-Data-Policy.md`](/docs/places-left-behind/game-data-policy).

## 14. Disclaimer

Submitted content is provided by the creator "as is". The studio does not warrant it, and users use it at their own risk.

## 15. Legal Review Requirements

1. Whether the licence in section 5 is appropriately limited and clearly drafted.
2. Whether click-through acceptance is sufficient, or a signature is required.
3. How the removal right interacts with the creator's own licensing.
4. Whether a separate agreement is needed for content to be included in the game itself.
5. Data protection obligations for creator contact details.
6. Confirmation of the intended jurisdiction, Georgia, United States.
7. Guardian consent for creators who are minors.
