## 1. Purpose

This license is intended to give mod authors clear permission to create modifications for Places Left Behind, while keeping the game, its engine, and its original assets under the owner's control.

It is written to be permissive about creativity and strict about redistribution of proprietary material.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Owner"** means Candler Shaw, operating under the public-facing name Next Step Studios.
- **"Game"** means Places Left Behind, including its code, engine components, and original content.
- **"Mod"** means a modification, add-on, map, location, model, texture, material, audio, music, gameplay change, or other content created by a third party for use with the Game.
- **"Official Tools"** means any modding tools, SDK, editor, or documentation the Owner publishes for creating Mods.
- **"Official Channels"** means distribution or showcase locations operated or endorsed by the Owner.
- **"Game Assets"** means the Game's original models, textures, materials, audio, music, writing, code, and other content.

## 3. Permission to Create Mods

Subject to this license, the Owner grants you a **worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, revocable** permission to:

1. create Mods for the Game;
2. use the Official Tools, if and when they are published, to create Mods;
3. use, test, and run your Mods privately;
4. distribute your Mods, subject to the [Mod Distribution Policy](/docs/places-left-behind/mod-distribution-policy);
5. share screenshots and recordings of your Mods, subject to section 10.

This permission covers the creation of Mods. It does not grant a license to the Game's code, engine, or Game Assets beyond what is expressly stated.

## 4. Ownership of Mods

1. **You retain ownership of your original work in a Mod.** The Owner does not claim ownership of your models, textures, audio, writing, code, or design.
2. This license does not assign your copyright to the Owner and does not make the Owner a co-author of your Mod.
3. You are free to license your own original contribution on terms of your choosing, provided those terms do not conflict with this license or with the [Mod Distribution Policy](/docs/places-left-behind/mod-distribution-policy).
4. Where a Mod is a collaborative work, ownership among its authors is a matter for those authors.

## 5. What You May Not Do

You may not:

1. **Redistribute Game Assets.** Do not include the Game's original models, textures, materials, audio, music, writing, or code in a Mod, except as expressly permitted by the Official Tools or by written permission.
2. **Redistribute the Game.** Mods must not contain the Game, a modified build of it, or files that let someone play without a legitimate copy.
3. **Distribute material you have no right to distribute.** Music, models, fonts, and textures from other sources require their own permission.
4. **Circumvent the Game's protections.** Do not bypass licence checks, build identification, or technical protection measures.
5. **Ship malware or harmful code.** No code that damages systems, exfiltrates data, or installs unrelated software.
6. **Monetise without permission.** See section 8.
7. **Misrepresent your Mod as official.** Do not present a Mod as made or endorsed by the Owner.
8. **Use the Game's names as your own branding** in a way that suggests the Owner publishes or endorses your Mod.
9. **Enable infringement or harassment** through a Mod, including content that defames real people or distributes private information.

## 6. Permission You Grant to the Owner for Official Distribution

If you submit a Mod to an Official Channel — or explicitly ask the Owner to host or feature it — you grant the Owner a **non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license** to:

1. host, store, and serve the Mod files;
2. display the Mod, its description, and its screenshots on Official Channels and in promotional material for the Game;
3. distribute the Mod to users through Official Channels;
4. reproduce the Mod as necessary for those purposes, including format conversion for delivery;
5. use the Mod's name and your credited author name to identify it.

This license is:

- **limited** to hosting, display, distribution, and promotion through Official Channels;
- **non-exclusive** — you may distribute your Mod elsewhere as well;
- **royalty-free** — no payment is owed in either direction unless separately agreed in writing;
- **revocable by you** for future distribution, by asking the Owner to remove the Mod, subject to section 9;
- **not** a transfer of ownership, and **not** a grant of rights to sell your Mod, to relicense it, or to create derivative works of it for the Owner's own products.

The Owner will not use your Mod's original content in the Game itself without a separate written agreement.

## 7. Requirements for Mods

A Mod distributed publicly should:

1. include accurate metadata: name, author, version, the Game version it targets, and its dependencies;
2. state clearly what it changes;
3. include any required third-party attributions for material it contains;
4. avoid modifying files it does not need to modify;
5. degrade gracefully if a required dependency or Game version is missing;
6. not include redundant copies of Game Assets.

## 8. Commercial Use of Mods

The default position is that Mods may not be sold.

- Free distribution, including through platforms that show optional donations, is acceptable where the platform's rules and this license permit it.
- Selling a Mod, putting it behind a paywall, or bundling it with paid content requires prior written permission from the Owner.
- Paid commissions for private Mod work are a matter between the author and the client, provided the result is not distributed publicly for payment.
- The Owner may adopt a different policy for a future official modding programme, and would publish it before it took effect.

## 9. Removal and Takedown

1. The Owner may remove a Mod from Official Channels if it breaches this license, infringes rights, is malicious, is unsafe, or is incompatible in a way that harms users.
2. Where practical, the Owner will tell the author why a Mod was removed and what would need to change.
3. The Owner may remove a Mod on receipt of a credible infringement claim, following the process in the [Copyright and Takedown Policy](/docs/places-left-behind/copyright-and-takedown) draft.
4. Removal from Official Channels does not affect your ownership of your Mod.
5. If you ask the Owner to stop distributing your Mod, the Owner will stop future distribution within a reasonable time. Copies already obtained by users are outside the Owner's control.

## 10. Screenshots, Video, and Showcases

You may publish screenshots and recordings of your Mod. If the Owner features your Mod in a showcase, the permission in section 6 covers the display, and the Owner will credit you as the author. The Owner will not present your Mod as its own work.

## 11. Compatibility, Safety, and Support

1. Mods are used at the user's own risk.
2. The Owner is not responsible for damage, data loss, or malfunction caused by a Mod.
3. The Owner may decline support where a Mod is installed, and may ask a user to reproduce an issue without Mods.
4. The Owner does not guarantee that a Mod will keep working after a Game update.
5. The Owner may publish compatibility information but is not obliged to test third-party Mods.

## 12. No Warranty From the Owner

The Official Tools and any modding support are provided "as is", without warranty. The Owner does not warrant that the tools will be available, stable, or suitable for any purpose, and may change or withdraw them.

## 13. Term and Termination

1. This license applies from the moment you create a Mod and continues while you comply with it.
2. The Owner may suspend or end the permission for a specific author who materially breaches this license.
3. Ending the permission does not retroactively invalidate Mods already distributed lawfully, but does end the right to distribute new versions.
4. Sections on ownership, the license you grant for Official Channels, disclaimer, and liability survive termination.

## 14. Changes

The Owner may revise this license for future modding programmes. A revision applies to Mods distributed after it takes effect. Material changes would be announced before they apply, and would not retroactively alter the terms under which an existing Mod was accepted.

## 15. Legal Review and Open Items

This license requires review before adoption. Points to resolve:

1. Whether the permission to create Mods should be framed as a licence, a limited authorization, or a terms-of-use acceptance, and which is most defensible in the intended jurisdiction.
2. Whether the Official Channel licence in section 6 should be narrower — for example limited to the exact files submitted.
3. Whether a separate mod author agreement should be required for official distribution, rather than an implied licence.
4. How the removal right interacts with a Mod author's own licence terms.
5. Whether any consumer-protection rules apply to donations or paid Mods.
6. The intended jurisdiction, Georgia, United States, is subject to confirmation. The Owner is currently under 18, so capacity and guardian involvement should be reviewed.
