> **Future Draft.** Places Left Behind is currently a single-player game with no network functionality. Nothing in this document describes an existing service, and no one is bound by it.

---

## 1. Purpose

If the game later offers online features, those features need their own terms. This draft prepares them, so that adding a feature does not mean improvising a contract.

It would apply only to online services. Offline use would remain governed by the end-user licence.

## 2. What Would Count as an Online Service

| Possible feature | Would this document apply? |
| --- | --- |
| Cloud saves | Yes |
| Multiplayer or co-op | Yes |
| Leaderboards or shared statistics | Yes |
| UGC sharing or a workshop | Yes |
| Account-based progression | Yes |
| An opt-in telemetry service | Yes |
| A launcher update check | No — see the [Launcher Terms](/docs/places-left-behind/launcher-terms) |
| A single-player game with no network access | No |

No decision has been made about which, if any, of these will be built.

## 3. Accounts and Eligibility

1. Some services would require an account. The draft [Account Terms](/docs/places-left-behind/account-terms) would apply.
2. A minimum age would have to be set, and guardian consent required where the law demands it.
3. One account per person, with accurate information.
4. The studio may refuse or close an account that breaches these terms.

## 4. Acceptable Use

Users of an online service would not be permitted to:

1. harass, threaten, or defame another person;
2. upload malware, or content that infringes another's rights;
3. attempt to gain unauthorised access to accounts, servers, or data;
4. interfere with the service, including through flooding, scraping, or automated abuse;
5. impersonate the studio, its developer, or another user;
6. use the service for commercial purposes without permission;
7. evade a suspension or ban;
8. publish another person's private information.

## 5. User Content in Online Services

If a service allows users to publish content:

1. the user keeps ownership of their content;
2. the user grants the studio a limited licence to host, display, and distribute that content through the service;
3. the studio may remove content that breaches these terms;
4. the studio may disclose content where required by law;
5. the user confirms they have the rights to the content they publish.

Detailed terms would be in the [Community Submission Terms](/docs/places-left-behind/community-submission-terms) and the [Modding License](/docs/places-left-behind/modding-license).

## 6. Availability

1. The service would be provided on an "as available" basis.
2. No uptime guarantee, service level, or maintenance window commitment is made.
3. The studio may suspend the service for maintenance, for security, or because it is ending the service.
4. Features may be added, changed, or removed.
5. If a service ends, the studio should give reasonable notice and should explain what happens to stored data.

## 7. Data and Privacy

1. An online service would require a privacy policy covering the data it collects. The existing [Game Privacy Policy](/docs/places-left-behind/game-privacy-policy-draft) draft would have to be updated before any service launched.
2. Data collection would be limited to what the service needs.
3. Users would be able to request deletion of their data, subject to legal retention requirements.
4. No advertising, profiling, or sale of personal data is intended.

## 8. Moderation

1. The studio may moderate content and conduct in services it operates.
2. Moderation decisions may include removal, warning, restriction, or closure of an account.
3. An appeal route should be available. See the [Community Reporting Policy](/docs/places-left-behind/community-reporting-policy) draft.
4. The studio is not obliged to monitor all content, but will act on credible reports.

## 9. Fees

1. If a service is paid, the price, billing period, and cancellation terms would be stated before purchase.
2. Refunds would follow the applicable store or the studio's published policy, and any mandatory consumer rights.
3. No fee is currently charged, because no service exists.

## 10. Termination

1. A user may stop using the service at any time and request deletion of their data.
2. The studio may suspend or end access for a breach of these terms, or if the service is discontinued.
3. On termination, the licence to the user's own content ends, but the user keeps ownership of it.
4. Sections on ownership, disclaimers, liability, and governing law would survive.

## 11. Disclaimers and Liability

The service would be provided "as is", without warranty. Liability would be limited to the maximum extent permitted by law, with nothing excluded that cannot lawfully be excluded. The intended position matches the [Game Warranty and Liability Notice](/docs/places-left-behind/game-warranty-and-liability).

Online services carry risks a single-player game does not: outages, data loss, and the actions of other users. Those would need to be disclosed plainly.

## 12. Changes to These Terms

Material changes would be announced before they take effect. Continued use after a change would constitute acceptance, where the law permits that mechanism.

## 13. Legal Review Requirements

1. Consumer law obligations for a paid or free online service.
2. Data protection obligations, including for minors.
3. The terms needed for user-generated content and moderation.
4. Whether a minimum age is required, and what verification, if any, is proportionate.
5. Cross-border considerations if users are outside the United States.
6. Confirmation of the intended jurisdiction, Georgia, United States.
7. Capacity and guardian involvement, because the owner is currently under 18.
