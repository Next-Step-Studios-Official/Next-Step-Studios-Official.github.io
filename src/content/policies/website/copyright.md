## 3. What you may do

Without asking, you may:

- read the site and link to any public page;
- quote a short passage with clear attribution and a link to the page it came from;
- download policy documents, the command cheatsheet and the brand kit for personal, editorial or reference use;
- use the brand marks under the rules in the [press and brand guidelines](/press#guidelines);
- print a page for your own reading.

You may not, without written permission:

- republish a whole page or a substantial part of the site as your own;
- sell the content or include it in a paid product;
- alter the brand marks or use them in merchandise;
- use the studio's name or marks to suggest endorsement of something the studio has not endorsed;
- remove attribution or copyright notices from a downloaded copy.

## 4. Reporting infringement

If you believe material on this site infringes your copyright, email [nextstepstudiosofficial.contact@gmail.com](mailto:nextstepstudiosofficial.contact@gmail.com) with the subject **Copyright notice** and include:

- your name and contact address;
- the work you say is infringed, with enough detail to identify it;
- the exact URL or page on this site where the material appears;
- an explanation of why you believe the use is not authorised;
- a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them.

Please do not include identity documents, passwords or other sensitive material. Send only what is needed to identify the work and the page.

## 5. What happens next

The studio will review the notice and may remove or restrict the material while it is checked, contact the person who supplied it, or ask for more information.

No fixed response time is promised, and sending a notice does not guarantee a particular outcome. Frivolous or knowingly false notices may be refused.

## 6. If material was removed in error

If material you supplied was removed and you believe it was lawful, email the same address with the subject **Copyright counter-notice**, identify the removed material and explain why you believe it may be restored.

The studio will review the explanation before restoring anything.

## 7. Other complaints

Complaints that are not about copyright — impersonation, trademarks, privacy or a security issue — can use the same address. Mark the subject so it reaches the right place: **Trademark**, **Privacy** or **Security**.
